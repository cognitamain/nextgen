## Description

In addition to video elements, an image slider offers a visually engaging way to present multiple images in a slick carousel format. This component is perfect for highlighting key features, showcasing portfolios, or creating a visually rich storytelling experience.
<span style="background-color: yellow; font-weight: bold;">This feature is for Nextgen-v2.</span>

## Adding a Video Header component.


1. In the WPBakery editor of your page
2. Click the ‘+’ icon in the top right corner
3. Use the text search to find ‘Header Video & Image’
4. The Video Header Settings box will open out
5. From here Select Header type Slider from the dropdown
6.  Click on the option to add a new slide. This will open the settings where you can upload images.
7. Click Save and the component will be added to the page

## Pro Tip
> Ensure that your images are optimized for web performance to maintain fast loading times.

> Use high-quality visuals that align with your brand identity for a professional and engaging appearance.

> If using multiple slides, keep the content concise and visually appealing to maintain visitor interest.

> Regularly update your slider content to keep your website fresh and engaging.
