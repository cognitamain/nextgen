## Description

Adding a Video Slider post is a custom post type, similar to a Video Slider, but it offers more flexibility to modify the post page in the same way as a site page. To access the posts area, navigate to the admin area (wp-admin) and find the "Video Sliders" menu item.





## Adding a new Video Slider
1. Click "Add New Video Slider."
2. Give your post a title.
3. Below the content section, under the "Video Slider Section," add the YouTube video URL in the video field.
4. You can either add a new video category or select an existing one.
5. Save your post by clicking the "Publish" button.

{% include image.html img="testimonial_video_slider.png" style="wide" lightbox="false" alt="Alt for image" caption="New Video Slider Post" %}


## WP Bakery side changes
1. Open the WPBakery Frontend Editor on the page you want to modify.
2. Select the Video Sliders component in WPBakery.
3. Choose a taxonomy and click the "Save Changes" button.
4. Click the "Update" button to save your changes.
